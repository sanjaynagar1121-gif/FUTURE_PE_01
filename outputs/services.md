# Bean & Bloom Café — Services / Offerings

Enjoy simple café favorites for mornings, breaks, meet-ups and takeaway.

## 1. Coffee
From a quick morning cup to an afternoon pick-me-up, enjoy familiar coffee options in a relaxed café setting.

**Benefit:** An easy choice for your everyday coffee break.

## 2. Tea
Take a slower break with a warm cup of tea, whether you are catching up with someone or enjoying a quiet moment.

**Benefit:** A comforting option for any time of day.

## 3. Breakfast
Start your day with convenient breakfast options designed for a satisfying and easy café visit.

**Benefit:** A simple way to make your morning break more enjoyable.

## 4. Snacks
Need something between meals? Choose from café-style snacks that work well for quick visits and casual meet-ups.

**Benefit:** Convenient food for a busy day.

## 5. Desserts
Add something sweet to your coffee or tea with a selection of café-friendly desserts.

**Benefit:** A simple treat to complete your café visit.

## 6. Takeaway
Short on time? Place your order for takeaway and enjoy your café favorites wherever you are.

**Benefit:** Café convenience even when you cannot stay.
